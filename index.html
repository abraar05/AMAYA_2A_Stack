Methodology & data reference

How a device is resolved, and the known limits of this demo.

How a device is resolved

Each occurrence from every source tab is attached to its IMEI. The latest dated, non-conflicting occurrence wins and becomes the latest resolved state. Undated occurrences never anchor a trail. Brand and model conflicts surface in the Review Queue rather than being silently overwritten.

Movement conflicts

A conflict is two sources disagreeing on the latest terminal state for the same identifier. The Review Queue keeps them visible until a human approves or rejects.

Known limits

Nothing here calls the manufacturer. This view is synthetic, offline, and does not represent AMAYA inventory.
